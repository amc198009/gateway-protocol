A complete system for inner work — on your own machine.

Gateway Protocol gathers the Monroe Gateway Process tradition into a single, coherent practice loop: guided sessions and breathwork, frequency layering, journaling reflected by an AI Council, and an optional anonymous community — all local-first. VI · Why it matters

Sovereign by design. Honest by principle.

Yours, privately

Your data never leaves your device.

Sensitive check-in signals — mood, and optional on-device voice and camera-stillness reads — are processed 100% on your machine, consent-gated, and never recorded, stored, or transmitted. Only the derived numbers you choose to keep are saved. Full data export and import, always.

Whole, not scattered

One coherent practice, not ten apps.

Waves, frequencies, breathwork, journaling, AI reflection, community — in a single arc that meets you where you actually are today. macOS (Intel + Apple Silicon), Windows, Linux, web.

Adaptive, transparently

The Council shows its work.

Every recommendation arrives with a plain-language reason drawn from your own check-ins and history — never a black-box nudge.

Owned, not subscribed

The work belongs to you.

A one-time license. Your journal, your trends, your protocols. If the network goes away, the practice doesn't.

## 1) Brand system — use exactly
**Essence:** reverent, premium, cinematic, sovereign. A quiet luxury for inner work — not a busy wellness SaaS. "Owned, not subscribed."

**Palette (dark-field):**
- Background / void: `#02020a` → `#05050f` (near-black, deep space)
- Surfaces / cards: `#0f0f22` with hairline gold borders `rgba(201,168,76,0.20)`
- Gold (primary): `#c9a84c` · Gold-light (accents/headings): `#f0d88a`
- Silver (body): `#bcc2d6` · Text: `#ece7da` · Muted: `#928974`
- Use gold sparingly as the sacred accent; let the dark field breathe.

**Typography:**
- Display / headings: **Cormorant Garamond** (serif, light weights 300–400) — ritual, elegant.
- Labels / micro-copy / UI: **Montserrat** (300–400), uppercase with wide letter-spacing for eyebrows/labels.
- Long body copy: keep line-height generous (1.7–1.8).

**Motifs:** the **◈** glyph (use as a section marker / bullet), sacred geometry (Flower of Life circles, torus/concentric rings), a soft golden "biofield" glow/halo, starfield depth. Subtle, never gaudy.

**Voice & tone:** calm authority, second person ("you"), spacious sentences. Evocative but precise. Avoid hype, exclamation marks, and growth-hack language. Think Monroe Institute meets fine stationery.

**Do:** dark backgrounds, gold hairlines, lots of negative space, one idea per view.
**Don't:** stock "meditation" clichés, neon gradients, emoji-as-decoration, cramped slides, drop shadows everywhere.

## 3) How to use it — the practice protocol (turn into clear steps)
1. **Arrive** — a short ritual: one guided breath, set a single intention.
2. **Check in** — a quick, private mood check-in (and optionally a 10-second on-device voice or stillness read). It shapes your recommendation.
3. **Receive your attuned practice** — the Council recommends a wave + frequency + breath, with the reason. One tap to begin (or choose an intent preset: Sleep · Reset · Deep Work · Gateway · Manifest · Pain Relief).
4. **Practice** — enter the cinematic session; the field responds to your coherence as the breath settles.
5. **Reflect** — journal; consult the Quantum Mirror / shadow dialogue.
6. **Integrate** — review Signal Trends and your Gateway progress; let patterns inform tomorrow.

## 4) Why it matters — the importance (lead with these)
- **Sovereign & private by design.** Your data lives on your device. Sensitive check-in signals (mood, voice, camera-stillness) are processed **100% on-device, consent-gated, and never recorded, stored, or transmitted** — only the derived numbers you choose to keep. Owned, not subscribed.
- **A complete system, not scattered apps.** Waves, frequencies, breath, journaling, AI reflection, and community in one coherent practice loop.
- **Adaptive & personal.** It meets you where you actually are today, transparently.
- **Honest.** It clearly separates what is *measured* from what is *interpretive* or *contemplative* (see guardrails) — trustworthy inner-work tooling.
- **Yours, everywhere.** Native macOS (Intel + Apple Silicon), Windows, Linux, and a web app; full data export/import.
